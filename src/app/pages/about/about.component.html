Our Story

Founded in 2020, Arova Growth emerged from a simple yet powerful idea: that every B2B company deserves access to high-quality, actionable data that drives real business results.

Our founders, experienced data professionals and marketing experts, recognized the challenges businesses face in today's data-driven marketplace. The gap between having data and having the right data was becoming increasingly apparent.

Today, we serve over 5,000 B2B brands globally, providing them with the data intelligence they need to connect with their ideal customers, optimize their marketing strategies, and accelerate their growth.
