The Challenge

TechCorp, a rapidly growing SaaS company specializing in project management tools, was struggling with inefficient lead generation. Their existing marketing efforts were broad and unfocused, resulting in low-quality leads and poor conversion rates. Despite having a superior product, they couldn't effectively reach their target audience of mid-sized technology companies.

Their main challenges included:

  • Generic email lists leading to irrelevant outreach
  • High bounce rates and unsubscribe complaints
  • Low engagement with marketing campaigns
  • Difficulty identifying decision-makers in target companies

The Solution

TechCorp partnered with Arova Growth to implement a data-driven marketing strategy. We provided them with:

Healthcare Email List

Precise targeting of healthcare administrators and IT decision-makers who were actively seeking project management solutions.

Data Enrichment

Enhanced their existing contact database with verified job titles, company sizes, and technology stack information.

Account-Based Marketing

Focused campaigns targeting specific high-value accounts with personalized messaging and content.

The Results

Within six months of implementing the new data strategy, TechCorp saw remarkable improvements across all key metrics:

📈

Lead Quality

Lead quality improved by 250%, with 85% of new leads meeting their ideal customer profile criteria.

🎯

Conversion Rates

Sales conversion rates increased from 12% to 18%, representing a 50% improvement in efficiency.

💰

Revenue Growth

Generated $2.1 million in additional revenue directly attributable to the improved lead quality and targeting.

⚡

Campaign Performance

Email open rates increased by 180% and click-through rates improved by 220%.
